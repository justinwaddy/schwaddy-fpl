What you are looking at
Four predictions of the same thing: points in the coming gameweek.
trop — this repository's model, availability already applied.
fpl — FPL's own ep_next, straight from the classic API.
b1 — season to date, shrunk toward last season (k=10).
b2 — mean of the player's last four played matches.
b1 and b2 are per-appearance rates, multiplied by the same availability the model uses, so no column is flattered by a player who scores well and rarely plays.

Caveat on the fpl column: ep_next is computed under classic scoring, not this league's draft scoring. The two tables overlap but are not identical — a keeper's goal is worth 10 here against 6 there, among other differences. Treat it as a good outside opinion, not a like-for-like number.
